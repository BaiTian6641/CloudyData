## Interpretation

- The native engine's interrupt path reaches DuckDB while a query is executing
  and leaves the connection healthy. This supports, but does not complete, S01.4.
- Import receipt phases and rollback logic are exercised at constructed commit
  boundaries. Real process kills and filesystem durability need a separate
  fault-injection probe.
- Requested launch bounds are not guaranteed. Layout must use observed metrics;
  caption geometry cannot rely on a nonzero caption-bar inset on this build.
- The local engine and import path can handle a 1M-row synthetic CSV within the
  measured conditions, with exact totals. It is not evidence for D2/D3, Arrow
  transfer, or interactive 1M-mark rendering.
