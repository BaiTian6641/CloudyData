# CUST13 rectangle/lasso selection evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Requirements:** CUST13, F39, Q21, S11
**Status:** implemented_unverified — final renderer/product runtime qualification
is blocked because device use was handed back to the owner.
**Prerequisites verified:** chart click/keyboard selection, native table
modifiers, exact source-row identities, selection overlays and View Data.

## Implementation

- A pointer drag over the offline Vega surface draws a rectangle and sends a
  revision-gated `LassoSelectionIntent` containing exact `sourceRowIndex`
  identities and an additive flag. A new press must exceed an 8 px threshold;
  a completed non-additive rectangle with no intersecting marks clears the set.
- Hit testing walks the Vega scenegraph for `cloudyBar` and `cloudyHit` items.
  Scene bounds are objects (`x1,y1,x2,y2`); symbol items fall back to their
  center/size geometry. Coordinates are converted with the SVG screen CTM,
  subtracting `view.origin()` and `view.padding()`.
- Ctrl/Meta-drag adds to the set. The renderer records the modifier at pointer
  down and the host no longer expires it by wall-clock; a slow Ctrl-drag keeps
  TOGGLE semantics. `RowSelectionReducer.selectMany` replaces/clears or unions.
- Pointer-up hit testing now posts the same `SelectionIntent` as a mark click,
  removing dependence on browser click synthesis after a drag. Synthetic clicks
  are suppressed; the selected bar/point overlays are `interactive:false` so
  they cannot intercept later clicks.
- The accessible summary describes rectangle drag and Ctrl-drag; the native
  table, keyboard arrows and View Data remain the non-pointer alternatives.

## Limits / blocked verification

- The final coordinate fix, slow Ctrl-drag, empty-rectangle probe and
  product-level `WorksheetView`/`WorkbookScreen` lasso path were **not rerun**
  after the owner resumed using the physical device. `emulator-5582` also
  disappeared from `adb`; the surviving API36 emulator was not started further
  because it consumes laptop memory. Final instrumentation is blocked on an
  available device.
- A top-padding click regression assertion was removed because the probe's
  letterboxed position could not guarantee the tap landed in the 8 dp padding.
- No line/area lasso screenshot; rectangle uses their transparent hit symbols.
- Shift-drag is not additive; Ctrl/Meta-drag is the documented additive gesture.
- Persisted selection and drill remain absent; drill still requires a dimension
  hierarchy.
